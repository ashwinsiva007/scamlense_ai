# ScamLens Product Specification

## 1. Problem and Goals

### 1.1 Problem Statement
Digital fraud has surged drastically with the rapid adoption of digital payments (UPI), instant messaging, and online hiring. Fraudsters exploit social engineering techniques, creating realistic-looking fake job offers, phishing SMS messages, parcel delivery scams, and forged payment screenshots. 

Key challenges for users:
- **Linguistic barriers:** Many victims, especially elderly family members, receive deceptive messages in mixed languages and cannot evaluate technical nuances.
- **Opacity of AI safety tools:** Pure end-to-end "black-box" AI systems either hallucinate or declare messages "100% safe" or "definitely a scam", eroding trust and leading to false senses of security.
- **Privacy anxiety:** Users hesitate to scan financial documents or personal messages if the images are uploaded, retained, or used for AI training.

### 1.2 Product Goals
ScamLens is an open-source, privacy-first fraud analysis tool designed to help everyday users and families evaluate suspicious messages, documents, and screenshots without storing any user data.

- **Objective Explainability:** Provide transparent, verifiable red flags that directly quote exact suspicious text.
- **Linguistic Accessibility:** Native-level UI and explanations in English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिन्दी).
- **Family Alert Card:** Generate easy-to-share summary cards that users can forward to parents or relatives via WhatsApp/Telegram to warn them.
- **Deterministic Risk Scoring:** Separate perception (AI reading text/layout) from judgment (transparent rules and real-time verification).

## 2. Core Architectural Principles

1. **AI Reads and Explains; Rules and Verifiers Decide:**
   - The vision-language model (Gemma 4) is utilized strictly for OCR, entity extraction, visual layout understanding, and plain-language summarization.
   - The final risk score and band are calculated deterministically by an auditable, open-source rule engine paired with live reputation verifiers (Google Safe Browsing, URLhaus, domain age checks).
2. **Strict Quoting of Evidence:**
   - Every red flag generated by ScamLens must quote verbatim text present in the analyzed input or point to an explicit observable visual inconsistency.
   - No generic accusations without tangible quoted evidence.
3. **Calibrated, Non-Absolving Language:**
   - The system **never** marks any input as "safe".
   - The lowest risk tier is explicitly labeled **"no major red flags found"**.
   - Wording always states that a communication *"shows signs of"* or *"contains patterns characteristic of"*, never *"this is definitely a scam"*.
4. **Zero-Persistence Privacy Guarantee:**
   - Uploaded screenshots and pasted text are processed purely in-memory.
   - No images or text are written to disk, stored in databases, or logged in telemetry. Memory is cleared immediately upon response completion.
5. **Flexible Privacy Modes:**
   - **Cloud Mode:** Utilizes Google Gemini / Gemma cloud endpoints with zero-data-retention agreements for rapid inference.
   - **Local Mode:** Routes requests to a self-hosted local model server (Ollama) so that sensitive data never leaves the user's local network.
   - **Mock Mode:** Deterministic mock harness for testing, development, and offline evaluation.

## 3. Risk Bands and Classification

| Score Range | Risk Band | User-Facing Label | Meaning & Recommended Posture |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **0 – 29** | `low_risk` | **No major red flags found** | No typical fraud markers detected. *Reminder: Never share OTPs or bank passwords under any circumstances.* |
| **30 – 59** | `suspicious` | **Suspicious patterns detected** | Contains atypical elements, suspicious link structures, or unsolicited urgency. Exercise high caution. |
| **60 – 100** | `likely_scam` | **Likely scam** | Exhibits known fraudulent patterns (e.g. advance fee demands, credential requests, typosquatted links). Do not engage or send money. |
| **N/A** | `insufficient_info` | **Insufficient information** | The uploaded image is unreadable, excessively cropped, or the text is too sparse to evaluate. |

## 4. Signal Categories

ScamLens analyzes inputs across 10 structured categories:

1. **Money Demands (`money_demands`):**
   - Demands for registration fees, processing fees, security deposits, or advance courier charges prior to receiving employment, prizes, or parcels.
2. **Credential Requests (`credentials`):**
   - Solicitations for One-Time Passwords (OTP), UPI PINs, ATM PINs, internet banking passwords, or installation of remote access software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk).
3. **Urgency & Threats (`urgency_threat`):**
   - Artificial time pressure (e.g., "Account blocked within 24 hours", "Electricity power cut tonight at 9:30 PM", "Legal warrant issued").
4. **Too-Good-To-Be-True (`too_good`):**
   - Unrealistic compensation (e.g., "Earn ₹5,000/day by liking YouTube videos"), unsolicited lottery prizes, or guaranteed investment returns.
5. **Impersonation (`impersonation`):**
   - Claims to represent reputable organizations (State Bank of India, HDFC Bank, India Post, Income Tax Department, FedEx, Amazon) using unofficial channels.
6. **Links & Domains (`links`):**
   - Mismatched domain names, brand typosquatting (e.g., `sbi-kyc-update.top`), link shorteners (`bit.ly`, `tinyurl`), numeric IP addresses, or newly registered domains (<30 days).
7. **Sender Discrepancies (`sender`):**
   - Official entities communicating from generic public domains (e.g., `@gmail.com`, `@outlook.com`), mismatched SMS Sender IDs, or international country codes (+92, +84, +234) claiming to be Indian institutions.
8. **Document Quality (`document_quality`):**
   - Poorly composited seals, misaligned stamps, mismatched fonts, blurred signatures, or grammatical inconsistencies in formal offer letters.
9. **Payment Screenshot Inconsistencies (`payment_screenshot`):**
   - Digitally manipulated UPI transaction screens, mismatched font weights in amount fields, missing UTR / Bank Reference Numbers, or spoofed payment success banners.
10. **Reducers (`reducers`):**
    - Mitigating signals that reduce the risk score, such as standard transactional debit SMS containing masked account numbers (`XX1234`), absence of hyperlinks, or instructions directing the user to official mobile apps rather than external URLs.

## 5. Supported Languages

- **User Interface (UI) & Generated Explanations:**
  - English (`en`)
  - Tamil (`ta` - தமிழ்)
  - Hindi (`hi` - हिन्दी)
- **Input Content:**
  - Any language or mixed script (e.g., Hinglish, Tanglish, Romanized Tamil/Hindi). The extractor translates and normalizes semantic entities before rule matching.

---

## 6. Scope Boundaries

### In Scope
- Single-message or single-screenshot stateless analysis.
- Multi-tier rule evaluation with quoted evidence.
- Live URL and domain reputation verifications.
- Plain-language explanation generation.
- Sharable Family Alert card generation (formatted for social messaging).
- Cloud and local execution modes.

### Out of Scope
- User account creation, profiles, or authentication.
- Persistent databases or upload history storage.
- Payment gateway integrations or monetization.
- Native mobile application wrappers (responsive progressive web application only).
- Active counter-fraud intervention (e.g., honeypots or spamming attackers).

## 7. Mandatory Legal Disclaimers

Every analysis report, UI view, and Family Alert card must display the following disclaimer text:

> **Legal Disclaimer:**
> *ScamLens is an automated open-source informational tool developed for educational and awareness purposes. It highlights common indicators and red flags associated with digital deception. ScamLens does not provide legal, financial, or cybersecurity guarantees. The absence of detected red flags does not certify that any message, document, or transaction is genuine or safe. Always verify transactions and official communications through authorized bank branches, official customer service numbers, or verified institutional portals.*

## 8. Reporting Cybercrime in India

Users encountering fraudulent communications are guided to official Indian regulatory and enforcement authorities:

1. **National Cybercrime Reporting Portal:**
   - Web: [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) *(Link status: to be verified)*
   - Scope: Official government portal for filing complaints on cyber financial fraud, impersonation, and identity theft.
2. **National Cyber Fraud Helpline:**
   - Phone: **1930** (Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System)
   - Scope: Immediate intervention for financial freeze in digital banking and UPI scams within the "golden hour".
3. **DoT Sanchar Saathi Portal:**
   - Web: [sancharsaathi.gov.in](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in) *(Link status: to be verified)*
   - **Chakshu (चक्षु):** Facility for citizens to report suspected fraudulent SMS, WhatsApp messages, or calls.
   - **CEIR:** Facility to block and trace lost or stolen mobile handsets.
